01

What I Build

I design and engineer AI systems that operate at the intersection of structured knowledge and autonomous reasoning. My work spans multi-agent architectures, graph-based inference pipelines, and production ML platforms that ship.

  • Multi-Agent Reasoning — orchestrating agent teams that deliberatate over graph-structured knowledge bases
  • Graph-Native ML — building inference systems where the data model is a graph, not a table
  • Production AI — taking research prototypes to reliable, monitorable systems
02

What I Believe

  1. The graph is the model. Structured relationships carry more signal than flat feature vectors — design for them first.
  2. Agents should deliberate, not just predict. Multi-agent systems that can reason about their own reasoning are the next frontier.
  3. Shipping is a skill. Prototypes are hypotheses; production systems are beliefs. I build the bridge between the two.
  4. The best infrastructure disappears. If users notice your ML pipeline, you've already failed.
